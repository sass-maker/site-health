## Why

High Signal is a personally directed commercial product with web, API,
annotation, data-ingestion, and scheduled background paths. Its largest risk is
a false-green homepage while ingestion, enrichment, freshness, or data
integrity silently fails.

## What Changes

- Inventory web, API, annotation, ingestion, source retrieval, AI enrichment,
  storage, scheduled jobs, notifications, analytics, and deployment paths.
- Define acquisition, reading/engagement, primary CTA/conversion, meaningful
  return, freshness, and product-quality evidence.
- Require every recurring path to expose bounds, timeout, concurrency,
  idempotency/deduplication, retry maximum, start/success/failure/freshness,
  durable unresolved state, cost, and source provenance.
- Verify data ownership, reconstruction/backup, migration guards, API latency
  and errors, deployment revision, live health, and downstream staleness.
- Add only critical missing controls and connect sanitized evidence to Foundry.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `high-signal-automation-readiness`: Product funnel, ingestion reliability,
  data durability, cost, API, background-job, and Foundry evidence contracts.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Primary repository and owned High Signal runtimes only; EverythingRated is
