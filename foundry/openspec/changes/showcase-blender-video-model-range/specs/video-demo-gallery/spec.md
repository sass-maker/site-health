## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Blender range proofs

Every Blender gallery item SHALL play a reproducible Blender 5.2 artifact for its exact registered scene language, and the eight artifacts SHALL use materially different scene construction and motion rather than a shared two-dimensional fixture treatment.

#### Scenario: Operator compares Blender styles

- **WHEN** the operator plays the eight Blender gallery cards
- **THEN** the artifacts visibly distinguish cosmic, architectural, refractive studio, low-poly landscape, organic growth, kinetic sculpture, tunnel-travel, and paper-diorama treatments

#### Scenario: Blender proof is unavailable or mismatched

- **WHEN** a registered Blender proof source is missing, stale, or attributed to the generic fixture renderer
- **THEN** strict preview-pack validation fails with the exact variant identifier

### Requirement: Local video-model range proofs

Every coherent-local-film gallery item SHALL play a real, rights-safe local-model artifact with an approved source keyframe and recorded generation evidence, and the three items SHALL demonstrate distinct motion intents rather than seed variation for one prompt.

#### Scenario: Operator compares local-model modes

- **WHEN** the operator plays strict, balanced, and experimental local-film cards
- **THEN** the artifacts visibly demonstrate controlled cinematic continuity, object or product motion, and stylized transformation respectively

#### Scenario: Local-model proof lacks evidence

- **WHEN** a proof is missing its keyframe hash, prompt, model revision, output hash, or real-proof posture
- **THEN** strict preview-pack validation fails rather than presenting it as a completed model proof
