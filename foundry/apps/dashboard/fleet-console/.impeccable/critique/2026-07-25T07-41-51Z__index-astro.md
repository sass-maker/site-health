## Anti-patterns verdict

The starting implementation had a moderate generated-ops risk: a manifesto-size
hero, repeated uppercase eyebrows, glow status, and square counters. The final
Founder surface keeps the dark Fleet identity but reduces the hero, quiets
section labels, uses a humanist system face, and leads with owner decisions.
It does not use invented metrics, generic hero cards, glass panels, or agent
theatre.

The deterministic scan now reports only the separate Marketing page's legacy
Inter declaration and its intentional single-font rule. Build was replaced by an
Activity redirect. Marketing is explicitly owned by the parallel AI-visibility
slice and is not silently presented as finished here.

## Overall impression

The interface now feels like a credible private owner workspace. Its strongest
quality is restraint: it says when evidence is absent, never fills silence with
fiction, and puts decisions ahead of provider status. The remaining opportunity
is speed for a repeat operator through keyboard accelerators after real usage
shows which actions deserve them.

## What is working

- Needs me exposes the actual bounded responses inline, with reversibility and
  mission context.
- Empty, stale, unavailable, and offline states are candid and recoverable.
- Mobile and keyboard structure is strong: skip link, semantic regions, visible
  focus, 44 px targets, live status, text-plus-color state, and reduced motion.

## Priority issues and disposition

1. **Resolved P0 — Needs me was a dead end.** Inline owner responses now write
   through the authenticated local control API, and failures remain visible in
   the decision card.
2. **Resolved P1 — The hero dominated triage.** The page header is shorter, the
   title is capped at 46 px, and tracking stays within the design guidance.
3. **Resolved P1 — Dynamic state was silent.** Projection slots now use
   `aria-live` and `aria-busy`; connection and action results use status regions;
   one global error supplies the recovery command.
4. **Resolved P1 — Mobile status and touch targets were weak.** Status retains
   text on mobile and navigation, brand, owner actions, and detail links meet the
   44 px target.
5. **Resolved P2 — Projects were a 30-card wall.** Projects now drill into
   details and group by attention, with secondary and parked work collapsed.
