## Why

The Explore Gallery names eight Blender styles and three local-model film modes, but their committed previews are generic SVG fixtures. The product therefore hides the range that is already available on this machine and implies differences that the artifacts do not visibly prove.

## What Changes

- Replace all eight Blender gallery fixtures with reproducible Blender 5.2 outputs that visibly differ in scene construction and motion.
- Replace all three coherent-local-film fixtures with real local LTX image-to-video proofs covering distinct motion use cases.
- Record truthful engine, renderer, source posture, hashes, prompts, and evidence for every upgraded artifact.
- Keep the existing 48-variant catalog, gallery interaction, exact preset handoff, filters, and style mixing unchanged.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `video-demo-gallery`: Require real-proof cards to play the registered renderer's output and require the Blender and local video-model lanes to demonstrate materially different motion capabilities.

## Impact

- Implements GitHub issue #125.
- Affects Reel Pipeline's gallery render scripts, committed proof sources, 11 MP4 artifacts, manifest, and gallery registry.
- Adds no production dependency, provider credential, publishing behavior, or catalog option.
