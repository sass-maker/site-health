## Purpose

Narrows the prepared non-iOS release slate to products with enough current
ChatGPT value while retaining evidence for reconsidering deferred candidates.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Low-value candidates are not planned for release
LoopTV, PostTrainLLM, and What It Takes to Win SHALL be classified as **not
needed for now** and MUST NOT appear in the prepared hosted-route, listing,
monitor, or submission-evaluation surface.

#### Scenario: Release slate is inspected
- **WHEN** an operator reviews prepared ChatGPT connections
- **THEN** the three products appear only in the eligibility record with the explicit not-needed-for-now decision

### Requirement: Remaining prepared candidates retain their contracts
SWE Interview Prep, SaaS Maker, and Drank SHALL remain prepared anonymous
connections with their existing fixed read-only contracts and independent
release boundaries.

#### Scenario: Prepared route registry is inspected
- **WHEN** the implementation is validated before deployment
- **THEN** all three retained candidates expose their exact approved tool catalogs and remain excluded from production-active monitoring
