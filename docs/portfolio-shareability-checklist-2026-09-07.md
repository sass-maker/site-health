# Usability and shareability checklist — remaining 55 projects

Based on the September 7 hands-on audit and subsequent pushed repairs. These are proposed acceptance conditions, not a claim that completing one item guarantees whole-product quality. The original 14 passing verdicts apply only to named public surfaces. Chess and Journal are excluded.

## Common acceptance bar

A target viewer can reach the experience, complete one useful core loop, understand the outcome and limitations, and use a readable interface without broken primary interactions. Qualify guest and account paths separately when both are promised. For local/native tools, include installation and persistence. A synthetic demo can qualify when labelled honestly. Public evidence must match the deployed version; green CI alone is insufficient.

Inactive experiments do not require an active roadmap. Private infrastructure can be usable without becoming public. Replacement recommendations are decisions to consider, not implementation requirements.
