## Why

The personal website, RolePatch, and Karte are public Toolbox surfaces that
should remain usable and quietly discoverable without becoming active product
commitments. Their automation should protect canonical identity, primary
activation, privacy, and lightweight experiment attribution.

## What Changes

- Verify canonical links and the four-product portfolio presentation on the
  personal website, with SaaS Maker as the directory.
- Verify RolePatch's resume-tailoring activation and Karte's public profile/
  trust-card activation without storing private resume, job, contact, or chat
  content in fleet reports.
- Standardize build/live/indexing/error evidence and the minimum meaningful CTA
  or activation event per surface.
- Add expiring, attributed, review-controlled quiet experiments using existing
  Foundry marketing and analytics facilities.
- Route only sanitized aggregate status to Foundry; no active roadmaps or
  automatic promotion into My Work.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `portfolio-identity-toolbox-automation`: Usability, identity, indexing,
  privacy-safe activation, quiet experimentation, and Foundry evidence for the
  personal website, RolePatch, and Karte.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Repositories: personal portfolio, `rolepatch`, and `karte`.
- No redesign, product expansion, paid acquisition, private-content capture,
  production deploy, or automatic portfolio reclassification.
