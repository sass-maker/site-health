## Use existing products instead

These are product judgments based on the observed Fleet scope and current official capability descriptions, not a hands-on benchmark of the alternatives. Only Chess and Journal removals are owner-confirmed. Other removals below are recommendations; their lifecycle remains inactive and their source/history is preserved.

| Fleet project | Recommendation | Why and replacement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chess | Remove — confirmed | The practice loop works, but rebuilding chess adds little distinctive value and the owner no longer wants it. Use [Lichess](https://lichess.org/features), with established play, analysis and puzzles. |
| Journal | Remove — confirmed | A reliable personal archive is more useful than maintaining another generic journal. Use [Day One](https://dayoneapp.com/privacy-faqs/), which documents encrypted storage and export options. |
| Office OS | Replace the general-purpose product | Use [Claude Cowork](https://claude.com/product/cowork) for delegated desk work or the existing [OpenClaw automation/task model](https://docs.openclaw.ai/automation/tasks). A visual office and agent identities are insufficient differentiation. Retain only a small outcome-ownership experiment if a recurring workflow exposes a concrete missing capability. These alternatives are not assertions of identical local privacy guarantees. |
| Local AI Video Studio | Replace the editor product | Use [DaVinci Resolve/Fusion](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) for deterministic editing and effects. Its narrow reproducible variant-comparison idea can survive as a recipe or extension, rather than another full editor. [ComfyUI](https://support.comfy.org/articles/9806306061-about-comfy) is relevant for generative workflows, but generation is a different job from this project's deterministic editing thesis. |
| Mobile Dev Cockpit | Do not revive | The owner already abandoned its required dependency. Use existing agent desktop/mobile task surfaces; retain pairing and approval patterns as reference. |
| Reader | Replace the generic reading app | Use [Readwise Reader](https://readwise.io/read) for articles, PDFs, RSS and highlights, or [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/support/pdf_reader) for scholarly PDF annotation. Resume personal reading before investing in another reading platform. |
| TrueHire | Retire the marketplace | A generic candidate-scoring marketplace has both trust and distribution costs; the observed public sample does not prove verified candidate onboarding. Use existing public work and direct evidence review rather than building another score-based hiring destination. |
| Verified Bases | Drop the marketplace until there is a buyer | A priced source listing exists, but its historical badges do not prove a working preview, purchase or delivery today. The marketplace creates an ongoing support obligation without demonstrated demand. If a real buyer appears, sell one documented, verified template through an existing storefront; do not rebuild marketplace infrastructure. |

The strongest additional “replace rather than build” calls are **Office OS, the generic Reader, and the full Local AI Video Studio editor**. Preserve distinctive research or small reusable components where they remain interesting.
