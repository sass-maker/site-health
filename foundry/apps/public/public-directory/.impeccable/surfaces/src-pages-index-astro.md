Scope: SaaS Maker public directory homepage. Visitor mode: Persuade with an
Experience-led first viewport.

Audience and job: visitors evaluating Sarthak's public products should
immediately understand that this is one accountable maker's maintained
portfolio, find the product relevant to them, and trust that the catalog is
real and current.

Primary action: explore the maintained products. Secondary actions: read the
latest learning, inspect the open-source package, or propose a serious external
build.

Proof and constraints: use the real generated catalog, canonical product
routes, domains, public history, and existing commission email. Preserve
navigation labels, routes, product data, legal copy, agent surfaces, and
accessibility.

Direction: a sunlit glazier's workshop rendered as a steel-framed glass wall.
One large clear hero pane and four colored product panes make the first
viewport memorable; specimen rows carry the dense catalog below.

Memorable moment: the visitor sees the studio story and four real products as
one architectural composition before scrolling.

Unresolved decisions: learning and legal pages retain their existing dark
reading world until they receive their own scoped design pass.
