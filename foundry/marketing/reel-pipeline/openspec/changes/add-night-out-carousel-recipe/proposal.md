## Why

The real Marketing maker has many production styles but no reusable party-story
format that opens with a spoken/captioned reveal and rapidly cycles through
themed images. The validated Night Out proof should become one real recipe
without introducing the broader model/theme-option system yet.

## What Changes

- Add `night-out-carousel` as one selectable recipe in the existing maker.
- Add a local renderer that accepts an operator-approved image manifest,
  creates the "You're not gonna believe this" hook, bouncy themed cards, end
  prompt, and original procedural funk bed, and returns a real 9:16 MP4.
- Keep the theme inside the approved asset manifest so anime, fantasy,
  superhero, or other concepts do not multiply the recipe catalog.
- Carry source posture and rights evidence into the render receipt. Named-IP
  assets remain valid for private concepting, while commercial preparation
  stays subject to the existing rights gate.
- Add one exact fixture preview to the Explore Gallery and grouped recipe
  selector.
- Leave model installation, model selection UI, and the broader theme-pack
  registry outside this change.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `marketing-video-studio`: Add a real Night Out carousel recipe driven by an
  approved local asset manifest and original local audio.
- `studio-web-ui`: Expose the recipe and its required inputs through the
  existing compact maker controls.

## Impact

- Affected code: Studio arsenal, execution registry, local renderer factory,
  maker execution handoff, gallery fixtures, tests, and product documentation.
- Runtime: existing Node, headless Chrome, and FFmpeg boundaries only.
- Dependencies: no new production dependency and no model download.
- Publishing: unchanged; this change renders locally and never publishes.
- Deployment: no deploy, release, credential, or production-config change.
