## Why

Research Papers and Starboard are data-heavy personal Toolbox products whose
value depends on search, indexing, refresh freshness, and reconstructable data.
They should remain usable and measurable without being treated as active
commercial roadmaps.

## What Changes

- Inventory public search/analytics surfaces, ingestion or refresh jobs,
  storage/index ownership, reconstruction paths, APIs, indexing, and deployment.
- Define meaningful activation as a successful search, result inspection, or
  saved/organized action rather than generic page views.
- Require bounds, freshness, retries, idempotency, failure state, source
  provenance, and data durability/reconstruction evidence for refresh paths.
- Add only critical missing controls and connect aggregate status to Foundry.
- Allow quiet, expiring discoverability experiments without autonomous product
  development.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `data-research-toolbox-automation`: Search activation, ingestion freshness,
  data reconstruction, indexing, quiet experimentation, and Foundry evidence
  for Research Papers and Starboard.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Repositories: `research-papers` and `starboard`.
- No corpus expansion, ranking rewrite, paid data source, production migration,
  automatic deploy, or promotion to My Work.
