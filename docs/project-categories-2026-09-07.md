# Fleet project categories

Owner taxonomy, applied 2026-09-07. The canonical field is `projects[].category` in `apps/backend/config/projects.json`.

- **utility**: a practical application, tool, service, or shared infrastructure.
- **media**: a publication, content collection, discovery catalog, or viewing destination.
- **experimental**: deliberate research, a prototype, or an unproven product thesis. This takes precedence over its potential utility or media use.

Category does not set lifecycle status or shareability. PostTrainLLM stays primary and Nomad Data Adventure stays active. Reel Pipeline and Forecast Lab remain non-shareable by owner decision. A qualified experiment may still be shared as an experiment.

All 57 retained identities are classified. The 54-project qualification scope excludes Chess and Journal (removed) and Nomad Data Adventure (separately retained). Classifying historical identities does not restore them to Fleet.
