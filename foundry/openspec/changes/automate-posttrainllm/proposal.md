## Why

PostTrainLLM is a personally directed Mac-local specialist-model factory with a
public product surface and local training/runtime workflows. It needs durable
release, activation, error, benchmark, and documentation evidence without
uploading private datasets, prompts, checkpoints, or model outputs.

## What Changes

- Inventory the Astro/public site, WebGPU playground, local training,
  evaluation, packaging/runtime, downloads, docs, feeds, scheduled refreshes,
  datasets, model artifacts, and deployment/release paths.
- Define acquisition, download/run intent, first successful local workflow,
  evaluation success, meaningful return, and privacy-safe failure evidence.
- Verify build, benchmark/eval, artifact provenance, scheduled data freshness,
  documentation/indexing, landing deployment, and source-revision evidence.
- Add only critical missing automation and emit sanitized Foundry receipts.
- Keep model quality claims evidence-backed and production deployment manual.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `posttrainllm-automation-readiness`: Product, local-training, evaluation,
  release, privacy, freshness, and Foundry evidence contracts.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Primary repository: `posttrainllm`.
- No private training-data upload, automatic model publishing, paid training,
  model-behavior rewrite, new production dependency, or production deployment.
