# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Curious Indian adults using a phone or laptop to explore how selective a set of
demographic filters is. They want a quick, entertaining answer, but they should
also be able to inspect the denominator, sources, modelling boundary, and
uncertainty behind it.

## Product Purpose

India Standards estimates how many Indian adults match selected demographic
standards. The experiment should make joint demographic rarity legible without
pretending to predict dating success, mutual compatibility, or an individual's
chance of meeting someone.

The local MVP proves the interaction and calculation architecture with an
embedded DuckDB demo model. It becomes survey-backed only after licensed PLFS
and NFHS inputs are imported and validated.

## Positioning

A playful demographic calculator that treats uncertainty and source limitations
as part of the result.

## Brand Personality

Playful, honest, and quick. Copy is conversational without becoming flippant
about statistics. Results should feel shareable, while caveats remain visible
and plain-spoken.

## Anti-references

- Government data portals with dense institutional chrome.
- Dating apps, compatibility quizzes, hearts, swipe mechanics, or match scores.
- Viral calculators that show false precision or bury methodology.
- India-themed tricolour, flag, or map decoration.
- Generic dashboard card grids and oversized isolated hero metrics.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the range, then immediately provide denominator and numeric
   range-precision context.
2. Make every filter fast to adjust on touch screens without hiding the live
   result.
3. Keep modelled height visibly distinct from exact PLFS filters.
4. Use delight in the interaction and distribution motif, not in fabricated
   certainty.
5. Keep the demo-data boundary persistent, not relegated to fine print.
6. Preserve joint-data accuracy and filter coverage before optimizing storage;
   compact the serving cube only with output-equivalence evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All controls must be keyboard-operable, have visible focus
states and at least 44px touch targets, and remain understandable without color.
Motion is limited to state feedback and respects reduced-motion preferences.
