# Inspiration research contract

## Search frame

Capture these before browsing:

- surface and primary user outcome;
- product mode: persuade, operate, read, or experience;
- incumbent direction and immutable brand/product constraints;
- desired qualities and anti-qualities;
- content, interaction, viewport, accessibility, and performance constraints.

## Candidate filter

Keep a candidate only when it contributes a distinct, transferable lesson.
Reject it when it is merely fashionable, incompatible with the surface's job,
dependent on unavailable content, inaccessible, or indistinguishable from a
stronger candidate.

For every retained reference record:

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Name | Product or original work name |
| Source | Direct URL and access date |
| Surface | Exact page, state, component, or sequence inspected |
| Lesson | One or two transferable principles |
| Avoid | Styling, content, interaction, or implementation not to copy |
| Constraint fit | Why the lesson fits this project and surface |

## Output

Return:

1. **Search frame** — one concise paragraph.
2. **References** — two or three entries with source, lesson, and avoid note.
3. **Anti-reference** — at least one named pattern or example and why.
4. **Direction principles** — three to five project-native rules.
5. **Probe brief** — only when probes are requested; define materially distinct
   static or code probes without prescribing a screenshot trace.
6. **Decision needed** — owner selection, delegated choice, or no decision for
   research-only work.

Do not use a direction-probe slot to store a source screenshot. Do not claim a
reference is current, free, open source, or licensable without live evidence.

## Code-probe lifecycle

Use code probes only when static evidence cannot demonstrate the interaction or
responsive decision. Record:

- why working code is necessary;
- two or three probe ids and project-relative paths;
- the temporary comparison surface and how it stays out of production
  navigation, analytics, indexing, and normal user journeys;
- the shared content and evaluation scenario;
- selected probe and owner decision;
- cleanup evidence showing the comparison scaffold and rejected probes were
  removed, or the explicit product decision that retained them.
