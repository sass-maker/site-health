# Channel quality

## Flagship

Each flagship asset must have a clear audience, original angle, accurate claim
ledger, useful substance, native format, specific CTA, and appropriate visual.
Do not spray one generic announcement across flagship channels.

## Secondary

Broad distribution is acceptable only when:

- the product fits the destination's active audience and policy;
- the listing/post is accurate and uses destination-specific required fields;
- the same community is not flooded with duplicates;
- the account represents the real owner/product;
- there is no fabricated testimonial, vote, review, or engagement;
- cost and moderation state are visible in preview.

Low effort means concise adaptation, not irrelevant or deceptive spam.

## Registry reconciliation

`ops/config/directory-submissions/directories.json` is a lead list. Reverify
every retained destination live. Do not use its dofollow/DR claims as quality
authority. Convert stale automation labels to current `connector`, `postiz`,
`browser`, `manual`, or `blocked` modes with a timestamp and reason.

Exclude legacy `directory-submit` spray code. It uses hidden browser behavior,
force submission, and ambiguous success signals that violate this skill.
