## Why

Marketing Explore Gallery currently equates catalog coverage with showcase quality. It exposes 48 short videos because the maker has 48 exact variants, even when several videos differ only by palette and most are generic 2.4-second SVG/UI fixtures. That is placeholder coverage: it proves that a registry and media endpoint work, but it does not demonstrate what the product can create.

## What Changes

- Separate internal exact-option coverage from visible capability-proof coverage.
- Keep every exact recipe and option combination selectable in the Marketing maker and validated by Reel Pipeline.
- Show one primary 6–15 second representative demo per meaningful capability, plus a bounded range of materially different proofs where one clip would hide the engine's real breadth.
- Give image generation, local video generation, ASCII animation, and Three.js dedicated proof ranges with authored camera movement and distinct scene grammar.
- Consolidate palettes, voices, presenters, continuity settings, and other minor options into maker controls instead of duplicate gallery videos.
- Reject generic fixtures as visible gallery coverage and surface missing proof honestly.
- Preserve current Fleet Console navigation, filtering, style mixing, responsive behavior, and maker handoff.
- Keep Grok explicitly excluded until a valid approved artifact exists; its absence must not suppress the complete local-engine showcase.
