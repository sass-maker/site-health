# Fleet project categories

Reconciled 2026-09-12. The canonical field is `projects[].category` in `apps/backend/config/projects.json`.

- **utility**: a practical application, tool, service, or shared infrastructure.
- **media**: a publication, content collection, discovery catalog, or viewing destination.
- **experimental**: deliberate research, a prototype, or an unproven product thesis.

Category does not set lifecycle status, Fleet membership or shareability. Paused experiments remain in Fleet unless the owner explicitly removed them. Archived identities are retained in the private catalog to preserve history and resource attribution.

The catalog records 59 identities: 48 retained in Fleet (including the separately retained Nomad Data Adventure), and 11 removed identities. This inventory includes StorageDaddy and Slow SERP; it does not expand the historical 33-project readiness cohort.
