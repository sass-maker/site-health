## Design Specificity Verdict

The result is strongly authored for SaaS Maker. The near-black folio, copper
rules, oversized headline, square code specimen, and question-led split
composition are coherent with the established visual language. The hero gives
the real execution profile equal weight with the thesis, which makes it proof
rather than generic technical decoration.

The deterministic detector ran once and returned zero findings. Browser checks
found no page overflow at desktop, tablet, or mobile widths, and no console
warnings or errors. Mutable overlay injection was unavailable, so no visible
overlay was claimed.

## Overall Impression

The article establishes seriousness without hype. Its strongest moment is the
methodology band. The main opportunity was to bring evidence of specialist
practice forward and give the four-part method a more memorable structure.

## What Is Working

## Persona Red Flags

  context. The new proof line makes the authorial basis explicit.
- A distracted mobile reader initially had to discover horizontal code
  scrolling. The shortened specimen now fits the narrow layout.
- An accessibility-dependent reader had no current-location state and compact
  source targets. Both were corrected.
